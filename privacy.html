Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026 · Effective immediately

The short version: ConvertIQ collects no personal data, has no backend server belonging to us, and stores nothing outside your own browser. The page content you analyze is securely sent directly to api.groq.com for AI processing. That's it.

1. Who We Are

ConvertIQ ("we," "us," or "our") is a Chrome browser extension that analyzes SaaS landing pages for conversion rate optimization (CRO). This Privacy Policy describes how ConvertIQ handles information when you use the extension and this website.

If you have questions, contact us at: privacy@convertiq.app

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

ConvertIQ is designed from the ground up to collect no personal information. Specifically, we do not collect, store, or transmit to our own servers:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your overall browsing history or the URLs of pages you visit (except the specific page you click "Analyze" on)
  • Your IP address or location
  • Usage analytics, telemetrics, or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Payment information (transactions are handled entirely by an external payment processor, if applicable)

ConvertIQ has no backend database that receives or stores your user data. There are no user accounts created by our extension.

3. What Stays in Your Browser

The following data is stored exclusively in your browser using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. It never leaves your device:

  • Audit results cache — CRO analysis results are cached locally for 24 hours per URL to avoid redundant API calls and save compute.
  • Daily usage count — A localized counter that resets each day, used simply to enforce the free tier limit (5 audits/day).
  • User plan status — "free" or "pro," stored locally in your browser.

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome or by clearing extension data in Chrome's settings (chrome://settings/content/all).

4. Data Sent to Third-Party Services

When you explicitly click "Analyze This Page" in the extension popup, ConvertIQ temporarily extracts specific visible text from the page you are viewing and sends it to Groq's API on your behalf to generate the CRO report. The specific data sent includes:

  • Extracted page text: headlines (H1, H2s), subheadings, button text, meta descriptions, and a text sample (up to 1,200 characters).
  • Structural signals: whether the page has testimonials, pricing sections, FAQ, or trust badges.
  • The current active tab's URL.

This data is transmitted directly from your browser to api.groq.com (the AI provider). ConvertIQ itself never sees, stores, or logs this data. Groq's handling of data sent to their API is governed by Groq's Privacy Policy.

Important: Do not use ConvertIQ to analyze pages containing sensitive personal information (PII, health records, financial data, etc.) or post-login user dashboards. The page content extraction and AI analysis is designed strictly for public-facing marketing and SaaS landing pages.

5. Chrome Extension Permissions Explained

Google Chrome Web Store requires us to list exactly what permissions we request and why. ConvertIQ requests only the minimum permissions required to function. Here is exactly what we use them for:

  • activeTab — This allows the extension to read the URL and inject a temporary content script into ONLY the currently active tab, and ONLY when you explicitly click the ConvertIQ action icon. The extension cannot passively read other tabs or monitor your browsing.
  • storage — This allows the extension to store your daily trial count, Pro plan status, and cached audit results directly to your local Chrome storage space.
  • scripting — Used alongside the activeTab permission, this allows the extension to inject the text-extraction script into the active tab to grab headlines and CTAs. It does not permanently modify the webpage.
  • Host permission (https://api.groq.com/*) — Required to securely send the extracted text to Groq's Large Language Model (Llama 3.3) to provide your CRO Audit.

6. Website Analytics

This website (convertiq.app) does not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies beyond what is technically required to serve the pages. We do not use Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel.

GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) as part of standard web server operation. This is governed by GitHub's Privacy Policy.

7. Payments and Pro Plan

If you upgrade to ConvertIQ Pro, payment is processed by a third-party payment processor. We do not store or handle your payment card information. All payment data is governed by the payment processor's privacy policy. Upon successful payment, a signal updates your local browser storage to "pro".

8. Children's Privacy

ConvertIQ is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at privacy@convertiq.app.

9. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data onto servers we own, there is generally no data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. However:

  • You can delete all locally stored data immediately by uninstalling the ConvertIQ extension.
  • California residents: ConvertIQ does not sell personal information and is therefore not subject to CCPA opt-out requirements.

10. Data Security

ConvertIQ is architected so that your web page content never passes through any server we control. The only external transmission is directly from your browser to Groq's API over encrypted HTTPS. Chrome's storage.local API is sandboxed to the extension and is not accessible by other websites.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance. We will not reduce your privacy protections without explicit notice.
